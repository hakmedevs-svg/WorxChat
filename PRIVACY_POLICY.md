# WorxChat Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WorxChat handles information when you use the WorxChat application and its related services.

WorxChat is designed with privacy and security as important principles. We aim to collect, process, and retain only the information necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the service.

By using WorxChat, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, WorxChat may process different categories of information.

### Account Information

This may include:

- Phone number or other authentication identifier
- Display name
- Username
- Profile picture
- Account preferences
- Authentication and verification information

The exact information required depends on the authentication and account system implemented by WorxChat.

### Messages and Conversations

WorxChat processes message data required to provide the messaging service.

Depending on the implemented communication architecture, this may include:

- Text messages
- Replies
- Reactions
- Message metadata
- Conversation information
- Group information
- Message timestamps
- Delivery and read status

WorxChat does not claim that all message content is end-to-end encrypted unless the specific implementation and protocol provide that protection.

### Media and Files

If you choose to send media or files, the service may process the information necessary to deliver and synchronize those files.

This can include:

- Images
- Videos
- Audio
- Voice messages
- Documents
- Other supported files

---

## 2. Device and Technical Information

To provide and secure the application, WorxChat may process technical information such as:

- Device type
- Operating system version
- Application version
- Network information
- Connection information
- Crash information
- Diagnostic information
- Security-related events

This information may be used to troubleshoot problems, improve performance, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

---

## 3. Local Storage

WorxChat may store certain application data locally on your device to provide features such as:

- Conversation history
- Application settings
- Cached media
- Account information
- Authentication/session information

The exact information stored locally depends on the application's implementation and enabled features.

Users should protect their devices with appropriate device-level security.

---

## 4. Encryption and Security

WorxChat uses security measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the specific feature and implementation, these measures may include:

- Secure network communication
- Authentication protections
- Access controls
- Secure storage mechanisms
- Cryptographic protections
- Session management
- Abuse prevention

Security technologies and protocols may evolve as the application develops.

---

## 5. AI-Powered Support

WorxChat may provide an AI-powered support system called WorxChat Support.

When you voluntarily submit information to support, the submitted information may be processed to diagnose and resolve technical problems.

This may include:

- Problem descriptions
- Screenshots
- Error messages
- Logs
- Relevant technical information
- Files voluntarily provided for troubleshooting

Users should not submit passwords, private authentication codes, API keys, private encryption keys, or other highly sensitive information through support requests.

---

## 6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

WorxChat does not intentionally request information that is unnecessary for providing its services.

You should never provide sensitive credentials such as:

- Passwords
- Verification codes
- Private keys
- API secrets
- Recovery codes

unless the application explicitly requires such information through a secure and documented process.

---

## 7. Information Sharing

WorxChat does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared when reasonably necessary to:

- Provide the service
- Deliver messages
- Operate infrastructure
- Maintain security
- Prevent abuse
- Diagnose technical problems
- Comply with applicable law
- Protect the rights and safety of users and the service

Third-party services may process information only when they are integrated into the application or required for a specific functionality.

---

## 8. Third-Party Services

WorxChat may integrate with third-party services.

Examples may include:

- Authentication providers
- Cloud infrastructure
- Push notification services
- File storage providers
- Analytics or diagnostics services
- AI services
- Other external APIs

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Users should review the privacy policies of third-party services before using features that depend on them.

---

## 9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The WorxChat mobile application may not use traditional browser cookies.

However, certain services or web-based components may use technologies such as:

- Session identifiers
- Local storage
- Authentication tokens
- Similar technical mechanisms

These technologies may be required to provide functionality and maintain secure sessions.

---

## 10. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

---

## 11. Account Deletion

Where account deletion is supported, users may request deletion of their account and associated information through the available account settings or official support channels.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for security, or otherwise permitted by applicable regulations.

Deleting an account may also affect access to conversations, groups, files, and other account-related content.

---

## 12. Children's Privacy

WorxChat is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable requirements, contact the official WorxChat support team.

---

## 13. Security Incident

Although reasonable security measures are used, no online service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

If a security incident affecting user information is identified, WorxChat may take appropriate steps to investigate, contain, remediate, and notify affected users where required by applicable law.

---

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When significant changes are made, the updated version will be published through the official WorxChat repository or application.

The "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this document indicates the latest revision.
